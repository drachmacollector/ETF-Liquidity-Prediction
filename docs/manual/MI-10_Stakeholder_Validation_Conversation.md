# MI-10 — Stakeholder Validation Conversation (optional, recommended)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Needed by phase | P11 (before pre-registration Part A is frozen) |
| Required or optional | Optional |
| Estimated user time | 30-minute conversation + 15 minutes writing notes |

## Why this needs you

The alert budget and useful lead time are business judgments. If you can speak with someone on the Mirae Asset ETF team (for example the person the brief was prepared for), their answers make the thresholds and the framing credible. Without it, the project uses documented defaults (≤ 1 HIGH alert per ETF-quarter outside episodes; 1–5 day horizon).

## Questions to ask (pick what fits the time)

1. How do you find out today that an ETF is trading at an unusual premium or discount? How quickly?
2. In October 2025 (silver) and February 2026 (crash), what information would have helped, and how many days earlier would it have needed to arrive to change a decision (e.g., pausing FoF lump-sum subscriptions)?
3. How many alerts per week across all ETFs would your team realistically read? What is an acceptable false-alarm rate?
4. Which products matter most (gold, silver, international, others)?
5. Who would act on what: product/operations, sales/distribution, risk/compliance?
6. What wording would compliance be comfortable with in an internal brief?
7. Is there internal data (e.g., creation/redemption activity, market-maker quotes) that could be used later?

## What to give the agent

A short note (bullet points are fine) in the chat or in `records/MANUAL_ACTIONS.md` under MI-10: answers, any numbers mentioned, and anything that should not be written in the repository (the agent will not record personal details beyond what you choose to share).

## What the agent does next

Translates answers into proposed alert budgets and horizon emphasis, shows you the proposal, and on your approval writes them into pre-registration Part A (A4) with "Source: MI-10 conversation, <date>". If you skip MI-10, the defaults stand and the report says they are assumptions.
